## Selected Project

### 01 — Metal Powder Feedstock Characterization for Additive Manufacturing

An individually prepared practical report examining metallic powder feedstocks and their relevance to additive manufacturing. The work brings together characterization and assessment methods including:

- SEM/EDS for powder morphology and elemental analysis.
- XRD for crystalline-phase assessment.
- Laser diffraction and dry sieving for particle-size distribution.
- Hall flow testing and powder flowability assessment.
- Density and powder-packing measurements.
- Interpretation of powder characteristics in relation to potential AM processing routes.
